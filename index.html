Welcome

Hi and welcome to my website “The Wendy House.”

Based in Berkshire, I’m a Counselling Psychologist with more than 20 years’ experience working in private practice, in schools and for various Employee Assistance Programmes.

I started off my working life as a teacher, then re-trained during mid-life.

To find out more about the therapy I practise, you can see my profile on Counselling Directory.
